How to use this planner

Start on Today. Today's workout and anything overdue, nothing else demanding attention.

Plan loosely. Fill the Weekly Plan when you feel like it — a blank day is not a failure, it's a rest day.

Bank the moves you like. Exercise Bank holds the moves you actually do, and one tap adds one to today's plan.

Missed a day? Nothing happened. Overdue moves wait in "Pick It Back Up" — no red, no streaks, no guilt. Do it when you can, or move it to today.
